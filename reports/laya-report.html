Method and caveats

  • The suites came from instax-dutta/sysone-bench and ran through its own run_suite scoring. A small wrapper, run_subset.py, picked five suites and left the repo's code unchanged.
  • Suites run: triage, guardrails, moderation (curated) and banking77_12, multilingual_intent (public). Skipped: agnews, mnli, emotion, sst5, and the speed-scaling test.
  • The Jev columns are copied from results/run_jev-1.13.0_20260921-212125.json in that repo. Jev was not called from this machine, and its latency includes the network round trip to TypeSafe's API.
  • Samples are small (25–160 decisions per suite). With 25 cases, one answer moves accuracy by 4 points, so read the gaps as direction, not decimals.
  • The multilingual checkpoint was scored only on multilingual_intent. It's a smaller model (322M vs 421M), which is why it's faster, and it wasn't tested on the English suites.
  • Both Laya models are stock, not fine-tuned. Reviews report fine-tuning on your own data is where Laya's accuracy moves most.
